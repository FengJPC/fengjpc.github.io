Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the privately operated FENGJ Nextcloud Mail service.

Data accessed and how it is used

When you connect Gmail, Nextcloud Mail requests Google OAuth permission to access your mailbox using IMAP and SMTP. Nextcloud Mail uses this access to show your mail and folders, synchronize mailbox changes, and perform mail operations you request, such as sending a message. Mail data can include addresses, message headers, content, attachments, and folder information.

Storage and sharing

The private Nextcloud installation stores the tokens needed to maintain the connection. It also stores the account configuration and caches synchronized mail data to support mailbox access. Your mail is visible through your authenticated Nextcloud account and to services you explicitly authorize to access it. If you send a message, its content is delivered to the recipients you specify. Gmail data is used to provide mail functionality. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can remove the account from Nextcloud Mail and revoke this app’s access under connections in your Google Account. To ask about retained service data or request its removal, contact the operator at the support address shown on the consent screen.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact the operator at the support address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
