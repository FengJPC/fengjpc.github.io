FENGJ Nextcloud Mail is a privately operated mail client for authorized use. Connect only mail accounts that you own or have permission to manage. You are responsible for actions taken through your account and for complying with your email provider’s rules.

Access and availability

The service relies on your email provider’s availability and permissions. You can disconnect any connected account in Nextcloud Mail. For Google accounts you can also revoke access through your Google Account settings.

See the Privacy Policy for information about data access, storage, and removal.
