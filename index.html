FENGJ Nextcloud Mail

A private webmail service for authorized users.

FENGJ Nextcloud Mail is a privately operated installation of Nextcloud Mail. It lets authorized users read, organize, and send mail from accounts they choose to connect.

Connecting a Google Account

Google OAuth lets you authorize Nextcloud Mail to access your Gmail mailbox through IMAP and SMTP. That access is used to synchronize mail and folders and to send mail when you choose to do so. You manage the connection through your Nextcloud Mail account settings and can revoke it from your Google Account.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data handling, and the Terms of Service for the conditions of use.

Contact the operator at the support address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
